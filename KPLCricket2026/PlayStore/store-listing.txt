APP NAME (30 chars max)
KPL Cricket 2026

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
3D T20 franchise cricket: bat, bowl, field and win the KPL title.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Lead your franchise to KPL glory in a full 3D T20 cricket game built for phones.

THE KPL SEASON
• Pick one of ten franchises and play a complete league season: Express (9 games) or Full (14 games).
• A points table with net run rate, then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the Final.
• Play every match yourself or simulate it - all other fixtures are simulated ball by ball.
• Chase the Gold Bat and Gold Ball and lift the trophy.

REAL CRICKET, BALL BY BALL
• Physics-based deliveries with swing, seam and spin, with a bounce marker to help you read the length.
• Bat with the stick and GROUND / LOFT / DEFEND buttons, or swipe your shots: cover drives, pulls, cuts, sweeps, scoops and huge sixes.
• Bowl pace and spin: yorkers, bouncers, slower balls, swing, googlies and arm balls - set the line and length and hit the accuracy meter.
• Fielders chase, dive and throw; take catches with a tap; run-outs, stumpings, free hits and super overs.

MODES
• Quick Match: 2, 5, 10 or 20 overs, day or floodlit night.
• 50 Challenges in five chapters, from first chases to death-over thrillers.
• Nets practice to sharpen your timing.

BUILD YOUR TEAM
• Pick your XI and batting order from a 16-player squad.
• Train players and hire batting, bowling and fielding coaches.
• Earn coins to buy better bats, spikes and fielding drills.
• Daily rewards, 26 achievements and full career stats.

LOOKS AND PLAYS GREAT ON YOUR PHONE
• Detailed stadiums with animated crowds, LED boards, big screens and floodlights.
• Four graphics levels (Low to Ultra), auto-selected for your device, with a 30/60 FPS option.
• Plays fully offline. No account, no ads, no data collection.

KPL Cricket 2026 is an independent game. All teams, players and the league are fictional. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any real cricket league, board, team or player.
