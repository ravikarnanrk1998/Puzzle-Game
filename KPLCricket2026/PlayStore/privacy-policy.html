KPL Cricket 2026 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

KPL Cricket 2026 ("the game") is published by RK Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It does not ask for an account, sign-in, email address, location, contacts, camera or microphone access. It has no advertising and no analytics SDK.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, stats, tournament, challenges, purchases made with in-game coins) and your settings are saved only on your device, in the game's private storage. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this information.

Permissions

The game does not request internet access. It may use the vibration motor for haptic feedback, and you can turn this off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
