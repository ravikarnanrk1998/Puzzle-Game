FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
Step into the floodlights of the Premier Cricket Clash - a fast, fully 3D T20 cricket game built for your phone.

🏏 BAT YOUR WAY
• Two control styles - pick in Settings:
  - SWIPE: swipe in the direction you want to hit, tap to defend, LOFT for big shots.
  - BUTTONS: aim with the joystick and press DEFEND, GROUND or LOFT at the perfect moment.
• A pitch marker shows the length as the bowler runs in - yorker, full, good, short or bouncer.
• A timing ring and PERFECT / GOOD / EARLY / LATE feedback on every shot.
• TV-style broadcast camera from behind the bowler, with a follow-cam that chases every boundary.

🎯 BOWL WITH CONTROL
• Choose stock balls, slower balls, in-swingers, out-swingers, quicker balls and mystery spin.
• Drag the target on a colour-coded pitch map, then stop the meter for accuracy.
• See exactly where the ball landed and your accuracy score after every delivery.
• Pick your bowler for every over and manage four-over quotas.

💰 MEGA AUCTION
• Take charge of one of 10 franchises with a ₹120 Cr purse.
• Retain up to 6 stars - every unused slot becomes a Right-to-Match card.
• Bid live against 9 AI franchises that value players by squad need and budget.
• Marquee sets, capped and uncapped sets, RTM drama and an accelerated round.
• Then take your new squad into quick matches.

🏟️ STADIUM ATMOSPHERE
• Day games under the sun or night games under the floodlights.
• Packed crowds in team colours, LED boards, big screens and waving flags.
• Full scorecards, partnerships, fall of wickets and Player of the Match.

⚡ PLAY YOUR WAY
• 2, 5, 10 or 20-over matches.
• Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty.
• Pause at any time - or simulate an over, an innings or the whole match.
• Plays completely offline. No ads. No in-app purchases.

Premier Cricket Clash is an unofficial fan-made game. Franchises, venues, kits and logos are original creations. The game is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any real cricket league, board, team or player.
