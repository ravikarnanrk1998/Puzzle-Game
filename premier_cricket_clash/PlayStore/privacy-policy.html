Privacy Policy

Premier Cricket Clash 2027 (Android app com.rkgame.premiercricketclase) · Effective date: 19 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Premier Cricket Clash 2027 ("the game", "we") handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online features.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings (such as difficulty, controls and sound) and your mega auction results only on your own device, so they are available the next time you play. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The game shows no advertisements, contains no analytics or tracking tools, and does not share any information with third parties. There are no in-app purchases.

Permissions

The game may use the device vibration feature for feedback during play. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
