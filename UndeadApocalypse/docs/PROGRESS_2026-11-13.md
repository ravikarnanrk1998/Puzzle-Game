## Bugs found and fixed

| # | Bug | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Popups (daily reward, confirm, store message, pause, revive) let the menu behind show through: the logo, buttons and bright ceiling lights were visible in the dimmed area and through the panel. | The project renders in linear colour space, where UI alpha blends in linear light: a 0.78 black dim only darkened the screen by about half. | `Ui.Dim()` converts the intended darkness to the right alpha. Popup panels are opaque. Dark translucent panels and buttons get the same correction (`Ui.Backing`). |
| 2 | In night and dusk stages, steel gun parts rendered pure black. | The gun metal is lit almost entirely by reflections, and the level's reflection probe is near black at night. | A small studio cubemap reflection probe travels with the weapon (`PlayerRig.ViewmodelCubemap`). |
| 3 | Posters and wall signs read backwards (for example "SURF" mirrored, "PLATFORM" reversed). | The texture U ran the wrong way for the side the plane is seen from. | Flipped U in `BiomeKit.Sign` and `Props.Lightbox`. |
| 4 | Aiming down iron sights: the gun body covered the middle of the screen, and a grey slab filled the lower half (shotguns, machine pistol). | The procedural receivers are as tall as their sights, so with the eye on the sight line they block the view. | For iron sights only, the eye sits 6-14 mm above the sight line and the gun is 3 cm further away. Scopes and red dot or holo sights are unchanged (`GunModel.HasOptic`). Visual only: bullets still go to the screen centre. |
| 5 | The flashlight blew out into a large white disc on nearby walls and zombies (Mall, Parking, elite stage). | Dimming checked only one ray at the centre and stopped at 40 %. | Five rays across the beam, plus inverse-square compensation like camera exposure (down to 12 %). |
| 6 | The HUD (objective, ammo, weapon slots) showed through behind the result screen. | The HUD canvas stayed on. | The HUD canvas is turned off when the result screen opens. |
| 7 | Boss Rush and campaign results said "STAGE FAILED" or "STAGE CLEAR". | One fixed title. | ROUND, MISSION, CHALLENGE or STAGE, plus CLEAR, COMPLETE or FAILED, by mode. The in-game announcement matches. |
| 8 | The button layout editor was see-through at the bottom: the Settings screen and toasts showed behind the buttons. | Its gradient sprite is transparent at the bottom. | An opaque backing under the gradient. |
| 9 | Test harness: the screenshot labels were one step late on Android. | `ScreenCapture.CaptureScreenshot` is deferred on Android. | `CaptureScreenshotAsTexture` at end of frame, written immediately. |

**Not changed:** gameplay, AI, damage, hitboxes, touch input and the save format. Fixes 2, 4 and 5 are visual only.

## Hold-to-throw grenade with a trajectory arc (version 1.0.7, code 8)

- **Touch:** press and hold the grenade button to pull the pin and show the arc. The thumb can drag to turn the view while holding. Release to throw.
  - The button glows orange while aiming, and "RELEASE TO THROW" shows under the crosshair.
  - Pausing, dying or the controls being hidden cancels the throw, and the grenade is kept.
  - Keyboard: hold G.
- **The arc** (`Player/GrenadeArc.cs`):
  - A red line along the exact predicted path: the same launch velocity, gravity, sphere-cast bounces (45 % speed kept) and 2.2 s fuse as `Projectile.Grenade`.
  - Red rings where it will explode: the blast-radius ring (5.5 m) with a soft fill, plus a small impact ring.
  - The line colour is in the material, because the URP unlit shader ignores LineRenderer vertex colours.
- **Throw:** the release throws from the same start point and velocity the arc used. While aiming, the weapon dips and the throwing hand is held low so the arm does not cover the arc. The one-tap throw path is kept for the autopilot.
- **Tests:**
  - Windows, `-uaPlan nade`: airport (open ground) and metro (walls and bounces), aiming level, down and up, two throws each.
    - Predicted vs real explosion point: 0.08-0.38 m apart (blast radius 5.5 m).
    - Cancel test: the grenade is kept.
    - 0 errors.
  - Android emulator, real touch (adb long-press on the button): the arc, ring, hint and orange button show while held; release throws (count 3 -> 2). In a narrow street the arc correctly showed the grenade bouncing off the wall back toward the player, and the explosion hurt the player there.

**Audio:** the game's own sound effects plus its own generated combat music as a continuous bed. No third-party music, so there are no YouTube copyright claims.

**How to regenerate:**
1. `Tools\ua.ps1 win`.
2. Run the Windows build with `-screen-width 960 -screen-height 540 -screen-fullscreen 0 -uaAuto -uaShots Builds\trailer -uaPlan trailer -uaShotScale 2`.
3. `Tools\ua.ps1 promovideo`, which encodes in the editor with `-nographics`.

**How the capture works:**
- `TrailerKit` fixes game time at 1/30 s per frame (`Time.captureDeltaTime`), saves each frame as a 1080p JPEG and renders the audio in step with `AudioRenderer`.
- The captions, title and end cards are a UI overlay.
- The editor encodes with `UnityEditor.Media.MediaEncoder`.

**Verified:**
- Windows file metadata.
- Frames decoded with VLC match the source frames (mean difference 1-3/255).
- Audio decoded with VLC: 42.5 s, no silent seconds, 0 clipped samples, peak -0.4 dBFS.

**Gotchas:**
- `Time.captureDeltaTime` does not change `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`, so UI effects that use unscaled time run faster in the capture. `WaitForSecondsRealtime` is real time too.
- `MediaEncoder` in a batch-mode editor with a GPU ran out of D3D12 upload memory after about 450 frames, so it runs with `-nographics`.
- The older `VideoTrackAttributes` "High" mode is only about 8 Mb/s and blocky; `VideoTrackEncoderAttributes` with `targetBitRate` fixes that.
