# Imported art: sources, licences and checks

All imported art is free, and its licence allows commercial use. Every imported model has a procedural
fallback: if the prefab is missing, or the player has a weapon skin or attachment equipped, the game builds
the original procedural model.

How it is organised:
- **Source files:** `Assets/UA/Art/Source/<site>/<asset>/`. Import rules apply automatically (1K textures,
  ASTC on Android, no embedded materials).
- **Prefabs:** `Assets/UA/Resources/UAArt/...`, built by `Tools\ua.ps1 art` (`ArtPipeline.BuildAll`).
- **Materials:** URP Lit, generated in `Assets/UA/Art/Materials/`.
- **Checking a model without building the game:** `Tools\ua.ps1 artpreview` renders every imported character
  (head front / side / top / back, full body) to `Builds/art_preview/`.

| Rig | Rocketbox bones are mapped onto the game's 21-bone zombie skeleton (`ArtBody`). The game's animation, AI and hitboxes run unchanged on that skeleton, and the imported body copies its pose every frame. |
| Textures | 2048 TGA sources reduced to 1024. Android: ASTC 6x6, normal maps ASTC 5x5. |

| Hair | Male_Adult_08's short hair is a set of alpha-tested cards laid out as rings and bars over a skin-coloured crown. On the infected walker those cards rendered as solid strips, a "cage" over a pale scalp. The cards are therefore left out of infected prefabs. The hair painted on the scalp texture is kept and darkened, using a hair mask built from the painted hair and from the head skin that lies under the cards. |

Infected treatment, generated by the pipeline from each avatar's own maps:
- **Skin:** pale, with thin veins, bruising and wounds.
- **Face:** sunken, bruised eye sockets and blood around the mouth and down the chin. These are placed from the avatar's own face bones.
- **Blood:** concentrated where a bite victim bleeds (chest front, neck, forearms, hands), only light elsewhere.
- **Clothes:** grimy.
- **Hair:** darkened.
- **Removed:** weapons carried by some avatars, and short-hair cards on infected avatars.

Type gear stays on top of the imported bodies as rigid parts on the imported bones:
- Explosive charges
- the Butcher's cleaver
- claws (Hunter)
- bone spikes (Mutant, Juggernaut)
- glowing pustules (Toxic)

### Card art (rendered in the editor, no new downloads)

`UA / 2b. Make Card Art` (`Tools\ua.ps1 thumbs`) renders `Resources/Thumbs/W_<weapon>.png` (every weapon,
factory finish, transparent) and `B_<boss>.png` (boss portraits from the Rocketbox boss prefabs). The store,
armory, loadout and Boss Rush screens use them, and fall back to the flat procedural icons when one is missing.
Re-run it after a weapon or boss model changes.

Required notice (to show in the game's credits):

```
MIT License

Copyright (c) 2020 Microsoft

Permission is hereby granted, free of charge, to any person obtaining a copy of this software and associated
documentation files ... (full text: https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/blob/master/LICENSE.md)
```

The full notice is shipped in `Assets/UA/Resources/UACredits.txt` and shown under Settings › About.

## Needs a signed-in account (download by the project owner)

These sources only allow downloads from a signed-in account, so they have to be downloaded by you:
- **Mixamo (Adobe):** free, rigged characters and motion-captured animations. Adobe's Mixamo FAQ says
  they are royalty-free for commercial games; the raw files must not be redistributed on their own.
- **Unity Asset Store free packages:** covered by the standard Unity Asset Store EULA, which allows use
  in games.
- **Sketchfab:** only models marked CC0 or CC-BY (CC-BY needs a credit line, which would be added here).

Put downloads in `Assets/UA/Art/Source/Mixamo/<Name>/` (or `.../Sketchfab/<name>/`). Mixamo files are
imported as Humanoid automatically. Each one is checked against the same list as the pistol above before
it is used.
