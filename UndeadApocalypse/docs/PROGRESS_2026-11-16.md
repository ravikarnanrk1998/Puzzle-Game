## Owner's requests

1. A minimap at the top left that shows zombies.
2. Drag-and-drop placement of the game control buttons.
3. The health panel to the right of the map, and a setting for how visible the game buttons are.

## Minimap (`UI/Minimap.cs`)

- **Placement:** a round map, 200 px at the 1080 reference height, in the HUD's top-left corner.
  - The health and armour panel sits to its right, level with it (`Hud.VitalsPos` / `VitalsSize`).
    - Its width (300-460) fits the space left of the stage banner, so the two never overlap on narrow screens.
    - At first the panel was placed below the map; it moved beside the map at the owner's request.
  - The default left fire button is at Y 0.58.
- **Map:** the stage's floor plan (open grid cells as floor with a light edge; walls and buildings left dark). It is painted once per stage into a texture, 12 px per 6 m cell.
- **Orientation:** the map turns with the camera, so up is always the way the player faces. A white arrow in the centre marks the player, and an "N" on the rim marks north. Range to the rim is 34 m.
- **Markers:**
  - zombies are red dots;
  - the boss is a red skull;
  - the objective is the gold pin, the same target as the HUD arrow.
  - Things beyond the range sit on the rim in their direction, smaller and faded, so zombies can always be found.
- **Not shown:** cloaked Hunters (new read-only `Zombie.Cloaked`), so the minimap does not give their position away.
- **Checks:**
  - The autopilot now checks every 2 s that the player stands on a minimap floor cell; 0.5 m is allowed at wall faces and the first second is skipped.
  - Boss stages: 44/44 and 30/30 on the floor, which confirms the map is not mirrored or offset.
  - Emulator screenshots: `Builds\shots_emu_minimap`.

## Drag-and-drop control layout

- **Where:**
  - new pause-menu button **CUSTOMIZE CONTROLS**;
  - still also in Settings > Controls > Edit layout.
  - A loading-screen tip mentions both.
- **Editor:**
  - It shows outlines of the fixed HUD parts (map, health, kills/coins/pause, weapons, ammo) and a name under each control.
  - The selected control is green, and the size slider is labelled "<NAME> SIZE".
- **Behaviour:**
  - Controls keep the point where they were grabbed and cannot be dragged off screen.
  - The **joystick** can now be moved and resized too: layout id "joy" in `Settings.Layout`, resting place plus size. It still floats to the thumb in play.
  - RESET only previews the default layout; SAVE stores it and CANCEL keeps the old one. Before, RESET wrote to the save straight away.
  - SAVE in game reloads the stage's controls at once (`TouchControls.ReloadLayout`); before, the new layout only appeared from the next stage.
- **Save format:** unchanged. The joystick is just one more `ButtonLayout` entry.
- **Tested** on the emulator with real touch (adb swipes): pause > Customize > drag the grenade button and the joystick > SAVE > RESUME, and the stage used the new positions at once. Then RESET > SAVE restored the defaults. 0 errors.

## Button visibility

- **Where:**
  - a **BUTTON VISIBILITY** slider (20-100%) in the Customize Controls toolbar, under the size slider, with a live preview;
  - the same setting in Settings > Controls, which applies at once if a stage is running.
- **Editor buttons:** SAVE stores it with the layout, RESET previews the default of 85%, and CANCEL keeps the old value.
- **Storage:** the existing `Settings.ButtonAlpha` field (default 0.85), so the save format is unchanged.
- **The percentage is what the player sees.** `TouchControls.PreviewAlpha` passes the value through the new `Ui.Fade` (a^2.2 in linear colour space), the light-on-dark counterpart of `Ui.Dim`.
  - In linear colour, a raw alpha of 0.24 on the light icons still looked about 53% bright, so the first build barely changed when the slider moved.
  - The 85% default now renders at raw alpha 0.70, very close to before.
- **Sliders on touch screens (all sliders):** before, only the 38 px handle took touches, so a swipe that started on the bar did nothing.
  - `Ui.Slider` now has a full-size invisible hit area and uses the new `TouchSlider`: tap the bar to jump to a value, drag anywhere on it to slide.
  - Inside a scrolling list (Settings), a mostly vertical swipe that starts on a slider scrolls the list and leaves the value unchanged.
- **Tested on the emulator** with adb swipes on the bar:
  - The slider went from 85% to 24%; after SAVE > RESUME the buttons and joystick were clearly faint in play.
  - RESET > SAVE brought back 85% and the default layout. 0 errors.

## Bug found by the minimap check: the player could be squeezed out of the level

- **Symptom:** in the Lab stage (Halls layout), at the dead-end corners of a corridor, the player once ended 3 cm inside the end wall and once 4.3 m below the floor behind it (and falling).
  - The old safety only respawned the player at the level start once they fell below -20 m.
- **Cause:** while zombies crowd a player backing into a corner, the character controller is pushed out of the overlap. Indoor wall colliders were only 0.6 m thick (facades 1 m), so the push could end on the far side, where there is no floor.
- **Fix:**
  - **Wall backing:** `Level.Backing` adds a solid block behind every wall face, 3 m deep and exactly one cell wide so it never reaches into open space. The thin face colliders still seal the corners. This applies to indoor walls, street facades and outdoor perimeters (forest, port/airport, concrete).
  - **Recovery:** a player below -1.5 m is put back at their last spot on the floor plan instead of the level start.
- **Verified:** autopilot plan `fall` plays the same Lab stage three times: 33/33, 35/35 and 35/35 on the floor, no falls, 0 errors. Before the fix, 2 of 4 runs went wrong.
  - Lab colliders went from 93 to 141; navmesh build time is still 24-42 ms.
- **Flight recorder:** the autopilot now logs the last 4 s of positions and the nearby colliders if the player ever drops below the floor.
