# Free gun models to download (Sketchfab, signed-in account)

Sketchfab only lets signed-in accounts download, so these 18 files need your (free) Sketchfab account.
Everything else in the art upgrade was downloaded without an account.

Every model below was checked on 6 November 2026 through Sketchfab's public API:
- **Download allowed:** yes.
- **Licence:** CC Attribution 4.0, so commercial use is allowed. The author is credited in Settings › About, which the pipeline does automatically from each download's `license.txt`.
- **Size:** 4,600 to 31,000 triangles. Each model gets mobile textures (1024, ASTC) and its own prefab, and the procedural gun stays as the fallback.
- **Brand markings:** logos and brand engravings in the textures (for example on the Glock or the Desert Eagle) are painted out before use. The game keeps its own fictional weapon names.

## How to download

1. Sign in at sketchfab.com (a free account is enough).
2. Open each link below, click **Download 3D Model**, and choose **glTF** (the "Autoconverted format" zip).
3. Save every zip, unrenamed, into this folder (create it first):
   `C:\Users\ravik\flutter soures\Puzzle-Game\UndeadApocalypse\Downloads\Sketchfab\`
4. Tell me when they are there. The pipeline recognises each zip from the `license.txt` inside it.

You can download any subset; guns without a download keep their current model.

## Rejected candidates

- **Stylised or flat-shaded models:** for example the sci-fi "AK-47S" and a flat-shaded FAMAS. They would not match the realistic look.
- **Models in a display case or with props baked in:** for example a Barrett M82 inside a case.
- **"Tactical Pistol - Fortnite":** a recreation of another game's asset.
